The models were converted from the packs' glTF/GLB files to vertex-coloured meshes by `prototype/tools/convert_models.py`;
the look is unchanged apart from the flat shading of the prototype's shader. Three place
settings are composites of pack models.

Six props once made in code are Quaternius models (`Tools/make_quaternius.py`,
`Assets/Resources/PropHunt/models_quaternius.bytes`), each fitted into the old prop's space: the dumpster (Toon Shooter
Game Kit `TrashContainer`), the chicken coop (LowPoly Farm Buildings `ChickenCoop`), the gas station's car (LowPoly
Cars `SportsCar`), the tyre stack (Zombie Apocalypse Kit `Wheels_Stack`), the wardrobe (Ultimate Furniture Pack
`Closet`, legs shortened, its two doors made to open) and the warehouse rack (LowPoly Modular Sci-Fi Environments
`Props_Shelf_Tall`, three of its five boards kept and moved to the old rack's heights).

Twelve small props are still simple shapes made for this project in the packs' style (owner decision Z5), because no
pack on the mirror has them: the tool wall, trough, mailbox, watering can, garden tap, mop, broom, garden shed, laundry
basket, folded towels, pair of shoes and fuel pump. The sounds are the packs' own .ogg files, used as stand-ins.

## Lobby (Udon world)

`Assets/Lobby`, all CC0 1.0 from the same mirror:
- Models: KayKit Board Game Bits (8), KayKit Furniture Bits (7) and KayKit Dungeon Pack (3), as FBX with their pack
  textures.
- Icons (each matched pixel for pixel against the mirror's previews): 256 px renders of pack models with the
  background keyed out: castle, church, tavern and home (KayKit Medieval Hexagon `building_castle_blue`,
  `building_church_blue`, `building_tavern_blue`, `building_home_A_blue`), bell (KayKit Holiday Bits `bell`), meeples,
  dice and hourglass (KayKit Board Game Bits `meeple_yellow`, `meeple_red`, `D6_A_yellow`, `hourglass`), crate
  (KayKit Dungeon Pack `box_large`); the lock is Kenney Game Icons `locked`, unchanged; the tile shapes `ui_tile` and
  `ui_round` are Kenney Board Game Icons `dice_empty`, recoloured. `ui_dashed` and `ui_dash` are drawn for this project.
- Sounds: Kenney Interface Sounds (`bong_001`, `drop_002`, `error_004`, `pluck_001`, `select_003`, `tick_002`) and Kenney
  Music Jingles (`jingles_NES03`), counted in the table above.
- Fonts: Kenney Future and Kenney Rocket (Kenney Fonts), with TextMesh Pro SDF assets made from them.
- The language board's globe (`Icons/globe.png`) is drawn for this project by `Tools/make_globe.py`. The letters the
  Kenney fonts lack come from Noto Sans and Rubik (SIL OFL 1.1, table above), cut down by `Tools/make_l10n_fonts.py`
  (`Fonts/L10n`, with the licence texts) and baked into TextMesh Pro SDF atlases by "PropHunt/Bake text table".

The translations (`Assets/Udon/Text/*.tsv`, 23 languages besides English) were drafted by AI (Claude) and have not yet
been checked by native speakers; corrections are welcome.

The on-screen control legend (`Assets/Udon/Textures/Legend*.png`) is drawn for this project by `Tools/make_legend.py`;
its key letters are set in DejaVu Sans Bold (DejaVu fonts licence, https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html).
The shields over the hider's health bar (`Assets/Udon/Textures/Shield.png`) are drawn for this project by
`Tools/make_shield.py`.
