The models were converted from the packs' glTF/GLB files to vertex-coloured meshes by `prototype/tools/convert_models.py`;
the look is unchanged apart from the flat shading of the prototype's shader. The wardrobe and the warehouse rack are
not from a pack: they are simple shapes generated by that tool in the packs' style (owner decision Z5). Three place
settings are composites of pack models. The sounds are the packs' own .ogg files, used as stand-ins.

Other software: Unity 2022.3.22f1; the VRChat world template and SDK (not part of the browser build).
